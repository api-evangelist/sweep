# Sweep


> Sweep is the agentic layer for enterprise systems. By connecting to platforms like Salesforce, Snowflake, ServiceNow, and more, Sweep gives AI agents the context they need to understand, plan, and govern changes safely across complex systems. Sweep connects discovery, design, and build into one continuous workflow, enabling teams and AI agents to analyze impact, plan changes, and ship projects in hours instead of months, without rebuilding existing systems or replatforming.
